2.5. Let’s recap!

A revision guide!

Religious vocal music (International)

  • Motet: a piece for several voices (usually 4 or 5), based on a religious text in Latin and with a predominantly polyphonic texture, which employs the technique of imitative counterpoint.
  • Mass: polyphonic music in Latin set to the most important prayers of this celebration.

Secular vocal music (National)

  • Chanson: texts in French, with melodic simplicity appealing to the common people, for four or five voices; varied themes (love, satire, etc.).
  • Madrigal: poetic texts in Italian, for four or five voices; usually on the theme of love.
  • Villancico: texts in Spanish. It was based on the alternation of two parts, known as the ‘estribillo’ and the ‘copla’, whose themes were usually romantic or picaresque.
  • Falas: texts in English. They contained musical sections based on the syllables fa-la-la

Instrumental music

  • Variations: based on a given pattern, the instrumentalist would successively vary the original melody.
  • Pavane: slow instrumental dance music, usually in duple metre.
  • Gallarda: fast instrumental dance music, usually in triple metre.
